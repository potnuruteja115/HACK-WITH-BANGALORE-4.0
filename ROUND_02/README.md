## 📌 Overview

The Problem Statement & Solution Discovery stage marks the beginning of our project's problem-solving process at **Hack With Bangalore 4.0**.

During this stage, our team will analyze the official problem statements released by the organizers, understand the challenges involved, identify potential users, and explore practical approaches to solving the selected problem.

Our primary goal is to establish a clear problem definition and a feasible solution direction before proceeding to system design and implementation.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the official problem statements released by the organizers.
* Identify the core problem and its underlying challenges.
* Understand the target users and their requirements.
* Research existing solutions and identify potential limitations.
* Brainstorm innovative and practical solution ideas.
* Evaluate the feasibility of proposed solutions within the competition timeline.
* Define the initial project scope and core features.
* Establish measurable goals for the proposed solution.

---

## 🔍 Problem Analysis

Once the official problem statements are released, our team will evaluate the selected challenge using the following approach.

### 1. Problem Identification

Understand the problem, its context, and the specific challenge that needs to be addressed.

### 2. Requirement Analysis

Identify the expected functionality, target users, constraints, and important requirements.

### 3. Research & Exploration

Explore existing solutions, relevant technologies, available resources, and possible approaches.

### 4. Idea Generation

Brainstorm multiple approaches and compare their potential effectiveness, feasibility, and practical value.

### 5. Solution Selection

Select a promising approach based on the problem requirements, development time, technical feasibility, and expected impact.

---

## 💡 Solution Planning

After selecting the problem statement, the team will establish an initial solution plan covering:

* **Problem Definition:** What problem are we solving?
* **Target Users:** Who will benefit from the solution?
* **Proposed Approach:** How will our solution address the problem?
* **Core Features:** What essential functionality should be implemented?
* **Technical Feasibility:** Which technologies and tools are suitable?
* **Expected Impact:** What improvements or benefits could the solution deliver?
* **Success Criteria:** How will we evaluate whether the solution meets its objectives?

> **Note:** The final problem statement, project concept, features, and technology stack will be documented after the organizers release the official challenges.
